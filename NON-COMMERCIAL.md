# Stance on commercial use of the software

Thanks for considering Tandem. This software is released under the Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial (CC-BY-NC)
[license](LICENSE). You may use, modify, and share it, as long as you do not directly profit from it. Tandem was created
to help people and teams collaborate around their mail (shared inboxes, assignments, internal comments and
automations) without the need for expensive third-party solutions. The non-commercial license is designed to ensure
that the software remains accessible to everyone, regardless of their financial resources.

Non-commercial use under the CC-BY-NC license refers to any activity that is not primarily intended for or directed
toward commercial advantage or monetary compensation. This means you can incorporate Tandem into your projects,
services, or operations without directly profiting from the software itself. However, generating revenue directly from
the software's core functionalities is prohibited.

#### Permitted Uses

You are allowed to integrate Tandem into broader services or use it internally within your organization without
charging specifically for the collaboration tool. This includes:

- Internal Operations: Utilizing the software so your own people and teams can work together on mail, support and
  sales conversations as part of your internal processes, without offering it as a standalone paid feature.
- Indirect Monetization: Including Tandem as part of a service that generates revenue from other aspects, as long as
  Tandem itself is not the primary source of income. For example, an agency or managed service provider that sets up
  Tandem for a client's team as part of a broader service agreement. You are prohibited to sell it as a standalone
  product or service.
- Service Integration: Embedding Tandem into a suite of services you offer, where Tandem is one component of a
  larger, non-revenue-generating package.

#### Prohibited Uses

Directly profiting from Tandem by selling the software or its core functionalities is not permitted. This includes:

- Selling the Software: Offering Tandem as a standalone product for purchase or licensing it to others for a fee.
- Charging for Access: Implementing subscription models or premium tiers where users pay specifically to access the
  team collaboration features or access to the software itself.
- Standalone Services: Providing Tandem's mail and team collaboration functionality as a separate, paid (hosted)
  service independent of other offerings.
